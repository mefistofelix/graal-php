DEVELOPMENT FAILURES RETAINED
The interrupted enum work initially had incomplete lowering and two failing
cases. The string-offset failure was corrected in the runtime, not bypassed
with a different target program. Initial native builds revealed eager frontend
IR initialization and a runtime-compilation boundary violation; both were
fixed in code, with failed logs retained separately from successful builds.
The Linux native regression command reached and passed all language/network/
cURL checks, then stopped because its FFI fixture filename omitted the 'lib'
prefix. The command failure is retained in linux-native-final.txt. The corrected
libffi-bridge-fixture.so invocation passes all remaining checks in
linux-ffi-final.txt; no product source change or failed scenario removal.

LIMITS
LANG-01, VALUE-01 and the entire design are not declared complete. Iterator/
generator protocols, attributes, unpacking, remaining builtin signatures,
Stringable coercions, typed-property references, full lifetime semantics,
shared-heap threading, complete FFI and GUI/mobile targets remain separate work.
WatchService tests use native temporary filesystems; no new /mnt/c guarantee.
No claims for macOS/mobile execution, a fully static distribution or Composer
application compatibility. JIT/GC defaults and compiler idle delay remain
unchanged. Build-resource summaries and network-test durations are not a new
RAM/CPU/throughput/p99 comparison. Historical performance evidence is untouched.
